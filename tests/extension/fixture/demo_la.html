About Corbel Residences

Set on a tree-lined stretch of the Westside, Corbel Residences pairs light-filled homes with an easy pace of life. Every residence features wide-plank flooring, quartz counters, and generous closets, and most homes open onto a private balcony or patio.

Spend weekends by the saltwater pool, catch the sunset from the rooftop lounge, or get a workout in at the fitness studio without leaving home. Cafes, grocery stores, and bike routes are a short walk away, and freeway access keeps the rest of the city within reach.

Pets are welcome with breed restrictions. Ask our leasing team about current move-in specials and self-guided tours.
