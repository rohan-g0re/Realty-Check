# Decisions

- 2026-11-14: harness model = Claude Code CLI headless Sonnet via own adapter (probe: 2/2 runs, 12–15 s). No API keys.
- 2026-11-14: UI = in-page edge tab + push-left drawer (Simplify pattern), not Chrome side panel (cannot be opened by automation).
- 2026-11-14: page reader is site-agnostic: regex + JSON-LD + one model call; no per-site selectors.
- 2026-11-14: single working tree, disjoint folder ownership, orchestrator commits.

## Wiring (2026-11-14)
- Floors are decided by measured roof height (aerial `height_m`) and county/building records only. The vision model miscounted the real demo building (7 vs 6), so a photo floor count is stored as `photo_floor_count` with `illustrative: true`, the judge ignores it, and it is not run at all once a roof height exists.
- Claim labels resolve to kit floor plans by exact, then word-boundary prefix, then substring match ("Plan E" → "Plan E 1 Bed + Den"). Whatever plan name the agent passes is mapped back to the claim label, so scan evidence and claims share one plan tag.
- "+ den" in the bedrooms verdict also comes from the scan model's title (e.g. "1BR W Den").
- Time budget: run target 55 s, agent budget 35 s; vision calls are skipped when under 15 s remain. Vision is not run on scan photos when the scan already gave room counts; scan stills are still fetched (cached) as proof images, after the agent too.
- Server: `RC_PORT` overrides the port (default 8787). Replays are capped at about 6 s and rewrite `report_url` to the serving server.
- Demo snapshots are invented listing text plus the public street address, in `tests/fixtures/public/`; `python -m server.demo_post <name>` posts one.

## Wider checks (2026-11-14)
- Floor plans: name match, else beds + baths + nearest size within 8% against the kit's plans; a kit plan without a scan borrows a property-level scan only when its title fits (beds, baths, title sq ft within 3%); otherwise the kit's own plan numbers are compared as `source="record"`, said in the reason ("according to the building record", "matched to the building's plan A2s by size"). A listing plan matching no kit plan is "not in the building's records". A kit plan's own scan whose title gives a size >8% off is not used (LA Plan F's scan is titled 1060 SF).
- Amenities are soft picture checks, presence only: one batched vision call on the aerial photos (top-down + 2 angled) for all outdoor amenities, one per scan (max 2 scans, room panoramas only, no schematic) for in-unit features. Seen ≥0.7 → confirmed; not seen ≥0.8 → close (a photo cannot prove absence), never contradicted; else can't check. No model call for things no picture shows. Answers cached by images + question (`rc_cache/vision`).
- Quartz and granite are asked separately: the model saw granite in the Houston B2 scan, and "stone counters" would have confirmed a quartz claim.
- A property-level scan titled after an amenity room (Fitness Center, Sky Lounge) confirms that amenity by name; when the aerial photo did not show a rooftop, the reason says it may be indoors.
- Picture checks start right after the address match in a background thread, in parallel with the agent; whatever is unfinished one and a half seconds before the 60 s budget is "not checked". Agent tools only report their status, so they never block the agent.
- After the agent, the fixed plan always runs as a completion pass (already-run checks are not repeated), so every claim gets its best verdict even if the planner skipped it. The inside checker now gets every floor plan in one task.
- Stories reason: "The measured roof height (23.1 m) means about 6 floors."
- Claim cap raised from 25 to 40 (the fixture pages have ~28); the claim model's parsed reply is cached by prompt hash under `rc_cache/claims`.
- LA aerial (2750 Corbel Ave): roof 11.7 m → about 3 floors (kit record also 3); top-down + 2 angled photos available.
- Scan area vs listed size: a precheck over 89 floor plans found the scan's measured floor a median 11% smaller than the advertised size, so the old 5% / 10% rule gave false contradictions. A size in the scan's own title ("… - 815 SF") is now compared with the 5% / 10% rule; otherwise measured area is confirmed from 20% smaller to 5% larger, close to 30% smaller or 10% larger, contradicted beyond. Record and county sizes keep 5% / 10%. The fake scenario's 968 vs 1,100 sq ft is now confirmed. (Measured band narrowed later: see False alarms.)

## False alarms (2026-11-14)
A run on 10 real listing sites gave 20 red rows, most of them false. Re-judging the saved runs offline with the rules below left 2 (from 20).
- Unnamed unit claims: "1 Bed" or "645 sq ft" with no plan name was compared with whichever plan's scan it happened to get ("1 bedroom, but the scan shows 2"; a penthouse's "3 bed" against a 1-bed scan). Now evidence about one plan or one scan can only confirm such a claim: confirmed when some plan in the building record (the run adds the kit's full plan list for this) or some scan title has those numbers, otherwise can't check.
- Scan room labels: labels gave "5 bedrooms" in a 2-bed plan, "0 bedrooms + den" and "0 bathrooms". A label count may now contradict only when the scan's title or the plan's building record gives the same count. Title or record agreeing with the listing → confirmed, saying the labels were not relied on; labels alone → can't check; 0 bathrooms from labels is never used.
- Near misses on building facts: year built within 1 year, or unit count within 2% (at least 1 unit), is close, not contradicted (215 vs 216 units, built 2020 vs 2019).
- Measured scan floor area: confirmed band narrowed from 20% smaller to 12% smaller (an 18% smaller unit had passed as a match); 12–20% smaller or 5–10% larger is close; beyond is contradicted. Scan-title and record/county sizes keep 5% / 10%. The fake scenario's 968 vs 1,100 sq ft is exactly 12% smaller and stays confirmed.

## Fill me in (2026-11-14)
- New `fill_in` event and Fact shape (CONTRACT 2.6, 2.7): things our sources know about the place that the listing does not say. Not a fact-check. Pure data shaping in `harness/fill_in.py` after the verdicts (a few ms per run; no model, no network). A failure there sends an empty list, never an error.
- The kit's renter facts (class, rating, style, pets, parking, schools, management company, vacancy, every floor plan, asking rent per plan) are not in the address index, so `python -m tools.kit.facts` extracts them once into one small file per building under `rc_cache/kit/facts/`; a run reads one file. Missing file = no kit facts, not a build.
- A topic the page talks about stays with the fact-check: claim kinds, claim plan labels and sizes, and keyword checks of the page text for topics the claim reader has no kind for (rent, pets, parking, schools, management, availability, …). When in doubt the fact is left out.
- 0, blank and Unknown enums are "not recorded" and skipped (vacancy 0, school distance 0, rent 0, units available 0), never "none". Record facts say "as of when it was last researched"; asking rents say "as of 2026-11-12" (the kit's listing date).
- Company names only: a name must carry a company word (LLC, Inc, Management, Residential, …) or it is dropped; no free-text kit fields (pet restrictions, comments) since they can hold contacts. No complaint data exists offline, so none is shown.
- Report: fourth tab "Not on the listing" (`#extra`), grouped by topic. Drawer: "Fill me in · N things the listing doesn't say", collapsed, top 4 facts, link to `#extra`.
- Match now carries `kind` (`building` or `home`) so a home listing never reads a building's kit facts (the two id spaces can collide).

## Claim field, report design A, short drawer (2026-11-14)
- Claim field (`server/claim_field.py`, CONTRACT 2.8): one column per claim, one row per source. A cell is the judge run on that one source's evidence alone, so the grid shows which source said what, not only the final verdict. States: match, contra, close, data (source has something on the topic, no verdict from it alone), none. Mismatched columns are highlighted. Hover shows a small preview of the data point; click opens a right-side panel with the pictures, the facts and the raw observed values. `clean_observed` drops names, contacts, free text, local paths and URL queries before anything is shown.
- Report page is now design A (`server/report_a.py`): headline from the counts, one module per mismatch with the best proof it has (3D walk, then pictures, then a measured drawing, then a record card), the claim field, the four sources, "Not on the listing", matches, couldn't check, questions to ask. The old tabbed page stays at `?v=old`. Not carried over from the old page: proof pictures and inline 3D on match rows.
- Drawer shows a short verdict: headline, counts, a mini claim field, at most 3 findings, "+N more", "Not on the listing (N)", then one button to the full report. Proof pictures and the per-claim list moved to the report. The drawer colours its mini grid with a simpler rule than the server claim field, so it can show fewer filled cells than the report; to fix, the server should send the cell states.

## Which address is the listing's (2026-11-14)
- A footer address (often the management company's office) was picked as the listing's. `detect.js` now scores each address by where it sits and what it is labelled: residential JSON-LD types, the main heading or top of the listing, a map link, the URL and street words in the title count for it; company/office labels ("corporate office", "managed by"), "nearby"/"similar" sections, Organization-type JSON-LD and footer-only placement count against it. A footer address is kept, not dropped: on a building's own site it is often the real address. The snapshot gains `address_candidates` (top 5).
- `server/address_pick.py` `pick_address` matches every candidate against the kit and prefers a residential property whose name agrees with the page title, web address or text (also the part of the name before "at"); footer-, office- or nearby-only candidates step aside when another candidate matches a property named like the page. No match: the best candidate with low confidence, saying so. On the 40 saved runs (one address each) it picks the same property as before; with an invented footer office address ranked first, 39/39 still pick the saved property.

## Floor plan names by structure (2026-11-14)
- Pages name plans generically ("1x1", "2x2", "Efficiency", "<Building> - 2 Bedrooms") or by size ("1BR 774 sq ft", "1 bed / 1 bath, 645 sq ft"), so a Houston run linked none of its 3 plans and its scans went unused. `tools/kit/plan_match.py` parses beds, baths, den, studio, sq ft and plan code from page names, record plan names and scan titles, and links in order: record name (exact, prefix, word), then the same plan code ("Plan 3K + Den" / "3K"; only when it does not disagree with the page's beds and baths), then (beds, baths) with the nearest sq ft (8%; 3% when the name itself is generic).
- A generic name that several record plans fit is a type-level match: no plan is picked; claims are checked against the range of the fitting plans ("1x1 from 650 sq ft" vs 640–720) and are confirmed or can't check, never contradicted or close. "from", "starting" or a range in the page's size never breaks the tie. A scan is attached only when it is the only scan of that kind of home, or the only one labelled as an example, and the reason says it is an example. Match gains `plan_types` (additive). Not yet wired into `harness/graph` (run.resolve / judge); offline on the latest saved run of 12 real sites, labelled bed/bath/size claims linked to a plan or plan type go from 184 to 216 of 219 (13 of them type-level), and to a scan from 81 to 101 (the Houston run: 0 to 5 of 5).

## Inline 3D in the report (2026-11-14)
- Every scan mismatch in report design A shows the live Matterport viewer in its proof slot, mounted by the page with no click and no new tab; the drawer's 3D button, the claim field panel's "See it in 3D" and the top nav go to that inline viewer (`#claim-<id>`), and match rows and panel cells without one open a viewer in place. Old new-tab links are gone from design A (`?v=old` keeps its own).
- The viewer opens at the picked spot, not the scan's start: the showcase URL gets `ss=` (Model API pano `label` + 1: the label counts from 0, `ss` from 1; label 13 opened sweep 12 in the browser), the SDK then only turns the camera, and the report keeps a still picture over the frame until the tour page posts "ready". No spot: the scan's start view and the card says so.
- The Showcase SDK key only accepts `localhost` pages (HTTP 400 "referrer is not allowed" from `127.0.0.1`, which is what the extension opens), so before this the viewer never moved to the spot from the drawer. `/tour` on `127.0.0.1` now redirects to `localhost`; the report talks to the frame by `postMessage`.
- Speed: preconnect to the Matterport hosts; spot picks start in one background thread when a run ends and when the report opens; the lead viewer mounts at once, others within 700 px of the screen, at most 2 live, unmounted 2400 px away. Chrome pauses a cross-origin viewer that is off screen or under a fade-in (opacity 0) parent, so a viewer mounted ahead of time sits invisible inside the screen ("docked") until its slot scrolls in, and its parent is shown at once. Headless Chromium with the GPU, saved LA run, cached spot: viewer at the spot about 9.5 s after page load with no click; before, about 10 s after a click on `localhost` and never at the spot on `127.0.0.1`. A first spot pick (picture check) takes about 30 s.
- The teammate's ListingProof report embeds a plain iframe and jumps with `ss=<label>` by reloading it (no SDK); its notes are in `rc_cache/ideas/listingproof_findings.md` in the private folder.

## Whole check inside deepagents (2026-11-14)
- The orchestrator now does the whole run in three turns: `read_claims` + `match_listing` (its own tools; the claim reading is one call through the same chat model, cached as before), `task` to every helper the claim kinds need (stories = outside AND paper; the list comes back with the claims), then `judge_claims` (fixed rules, unchanged) + `fill_in`. `judge_claims` refuses once while a needed helper was not asked. Subagents make one round of tool calls and end without another model turn (their report is the tool results).
- Vision goes through the same chat model: `ClaudeCLIChatModel` takes image blocks, writes them to a fresh folder and runs that call with only the CLI's Read tool. `ask_images` / `look()` use it; inside a run, photo checks are subagent tools and share the run model's deadline. No API keys.
- The fixed fallback plan is gone from the default path. Agent failure or timeout: whatever was gathered is judged by the same rules, the rest is "couldn't check", and `done` is sent; the agent thread is given up on at the hard limit and later events are dropped. `RC_LEGACY=1` restores the old flow in one step.
- Budgets: agent 75 s, run 100 s. Measured first (2026-11-14 saved runs): claims 7–23 s, orchestrator turns 3–12 s, subagent turns 3–5 s plus a 10 s summary turn, and cold scan stills ~8 s each fetched one by one after the agent (an LA building with 15 plans: 147 s). Now: no subagent summary turn, aerial photos fetched once per run, uncached proof stills capped at 3 per run, and photo downloads / photo checks waited for only until 12 s before the agent budget (later = "not checked").
- Real runs (snapshot + saved claim quotes, driven directly): the LA building 147.5 s (old path, fallback) → 55.6 s, all in the agent; a Houston building cold 88.5 s (budget hit on cold stills, judged by the close) → after the download cap 28.3 s warm, all in the agent, same contradicted/close counts as the saved run.
- `done` carries `timing` (total and per-phase seconds, model seconds per role) and `harness` (path, who judged, why the agent stopped).
- Plan linking uses `tools.kit.link_plan` in both paths; a type-level link can only confirm or "couldn't check" (`check_type_claim`).

## One switch for every result cache (2026-11-14)
- `RC_NO_CACHE=1` skips every read of a computed or fetched result so a run can be timed honestly: claims extraction (`rc_cache/claims`), scan summary, stills manifest and still images (`scan/<model>`), aerial raw responses and evidence (`aerial/<lat>_<lon>`), vision answers (`vision`), spot picks, spot scan data and skybox faces (`spots/<model>`), geocode and county lookups (`paper/geocode`, `paper/county`). `harness/graph/run.py` counts nothing as cached, so the per-run fetch caps apply to every scan. One helper, `tools/cachectl.py` (`enabled()`, `read_ok()`, `hit(path)` in place of `path.exists()`), read from the environment on each call.
- Writes still happen, in place at the usual paths: the report of a no-cache run serves that run's own pictures, and nothing older can be served because no read happens. A fresh per-run folder was not chosen: the run boundary lives in `server/app.py`, and a per-process folder would let a second run in the same server read the first.
- Not caches, unaffected: the kit dataset (`kit/`), HCAD bulk data (`paper/hcad`), saved runs (`runs/`). `spots.start_ss` (the viewer's `ss=` label read for the report page) also still reads the scan data on disk; it skips no work. In-memory de-duplication is unchanged (`server/tour.py` keeps finished spot picks for the server's lifetime). Unset: unchanged behaviour.

## Drawer: address line, same grid as the report, proof pictures (2026-11-14)
- Why the drawer and the report showed different cells for one run: the drawer coloured a cell only when that source was the final verdict's source, or for building counts (stories, year built, homes) whose own number equalled the listing's; everything else with data was hollow. The report runs the judge on each source's evidence alone (`claim_field._cell`, judge relevance `_usable` with fuzzy plan names), so e.g. a building record that has Plan E's 2 bedrooms is "match" in the report but hollow in the drawer, and a record's year 2015 against 2016 is "close" in the report. The drawer also kept only the last `claims` event; the report merges them by id. Now `done.field` carries the report's own states and the drawer renders them (close = blue, as in the report); its old rules only fill the grid while a run is going, for sample runs and for older checkers.
- The address the run checks comes from `pick_address` on the server; the drawer says "Checking: <building> · <address> · <why>" once `match` arrives, with "Not this? Pick another" listing the other candidates (address and building name). Picking one restarts the run with `address_locked`. Below 0.5 confidence the line says "We're not sure this is the right building" (the reason moves to the tooltip) and the list opens by itself, unless the person had already picked the address.
- Finding cards get a 72×54 proof picture again when the evidence has one, fetched lazily (in view) through the service worker as a data URL; a missing picture removes its slot. The card stays one finding per line block.
- Product name in the extension and server titles is "Realty Check"; code identifiers, storage keys and `RC_`/`rc_` names are unchanged.

## Report: ranking, quiet "close", plain panel, pictures back (2026-11-14)
- Mismatch modules are ordered by what a wrong claim costs the renter: beds, baths and size first, then rent and fees, then building facts (floors, year built, homes), then amenities; a bigger relative gap first within a tier (year built: 5 years weighs like a tenth). The headline counts hard mismatches only, the hero names the top-ranked one in one line, and it leads the findings. `close` verdicts come after every hard one as a quiet list with no strike-through and no big type ("Listing says 2015 · county records say 2014 — close"); only close ones: "Nothing is clearly off." The grid shows close cells and marks in a lighter blue.
- The evidence panel no longer shows raw keys or a raw JSON block: every observed key is turned into plain words ("Counted from: The title of the 3D scan of Plan E") or left out (scan ids, method names, confidences, picture counts).
- Proof pictures are back on match rows (one small picture where the evidence has one) and in the panel (compact, lazy, soft caption, opens the lightbox). Pictures whose cached file is gone are dropped before rendering (`server.report.drop_dead_images`, also used by the claim field, so the drawer's picture too); any that still fail are hidden in the browser, and an emptied proof slot shows its text.
- Under the grid, one line of short facts per source for aerial imagery (roof height, floors measured from above, footprint, things seen or not seen from above, photo date) and county records (year built, size, homes, floors, land use, owner type as "a company" / "an individual" only, 311 and complaint counts only). Only what the run's evidence carries; no new network calls.
- Fill me in adds the building's age next to its year built, typical size per kind of home (range across plans), amenities on record the page never mentions (kit `amenities`, when present), and 311 / complaint counts from county evidence. Fact rows name their source in plain text; CoStar is credited in text in the grid, the facts and the footer.
- User-facing name in the report is "Realty Check" (code names, env names and `rc_` prefixes unchanged).
- Not done here (other owners): the kit facts builder (`tools/kit/facts.py`) does not extract amenities, and no county tool returns land use, owner type or 311 counts yet, so those rows stay empty on real runs; the drawer still shows the run's own `done.headline`, which the harness writes.
