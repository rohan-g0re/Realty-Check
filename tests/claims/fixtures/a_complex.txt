About The Calloway on Brenner
Built in 2016, The Calloway on Brenner is a 5-story building with 100 units tucked between Memorial Park and downtown. Residents enjoy a resort-style pool, a 24-hour fitness center, and controlled-access garage parking. Every home features quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, wood-style flooring and an in-unit washer and dryer. Select homes offer private balconies with skyline views. The Calloway is pet friendly and welcomes cats and dogs.

Neighborhood Guide: Rice Military
Rice Military sits between Memorial Park and downtown, named for the Camp Logan training grounds that once covered the area. Today the neighborhood is a mix of townhomes, mid-rise apartment communities and restaurants along Washington Avenue. Weekend brunch spots fill up early, and the hike-and-bike trails along Buffalo Bayou connect residents to downtown in about 20 minutes by bike. Most streets are quiet and residential, with newer construction replacing older bungalows block by block.
Getting around: most residents drive, but bus routes on Washington Avenue and Westcott Street reach downtown and the Galleria. Rideshare pickups are quick, and parking at the shops along Washington is free on most side streets after 6 PM.
Things to do: run the Memorial Park loop, catch a game downtown, browse the Saturday farmers market, or try one of the many patio bars along Washington Avenue.
