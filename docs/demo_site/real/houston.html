About Us

Experience Downtown Living at 2111 Austin Apartments

Located in Midtown (one block from Downtown), 2111 Austin offers urban living at its finest. From 2111 Austin you can walk or bicycle to restaurants and entertainment in both Midtown and Downtown. With the bike lane and a bike station at your front door, you can walk/bike to Metro Rail Redline and Northline, Midtown Park, Discovery Green and Park and Root Square. Mere minutes from Bagby/Gray restaurants and bars and all that Midtown and Downtown Houston has to offer.

Built in 2018, the community has 184 apartments. 2111 Austin offers a variety of spacious floor plans: one, two-, and three-bedroom. Inside these spacious floor plans, you’ll discover finishes found only in custom homes, complete with all the modern conveniences you desire. Lavish 10-foot ceilings with custom crown molding, over-sized, double-paned windows with breath-taking views, and beautifully appointed kitchens with islands that boast wine/beverage refrigerators. Custom walk-in closets and hardwood-style flooring throughout the home.

Outside your home, the community’s inviting amenities provide the perfect opportunity to relax and unwind. Take a break in the sky lounge overlooking the downtown skyline or relax in the well-appointed clubhouse with Starbucks coffee bar. Indulge in the massive 24-hour athletic facility or chill poolside at the sports bar or in a private cabana. Host a dinner party or work from home utilizing the 24-hour conference room.

With its carefully designed amenities and outstanding location, 2111 Austin is destined to be the ultimate living experience. Reach out today for more information.
