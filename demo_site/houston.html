Overview

Larkspur on Marrow is a 5-story building on the edge of Midtown and downtown Houston, set back from the street behind a planted courtyard. Built in 2018, the community has 184 apartments ranging from efficient studios to three-bedroom homes, each with wide-plank flooring, quartz counters, and in-unit washers and dryers.

Residents get a quiet home base within walking distance of light rail, coffee shops, and the parks along the bayou. Our on-site leasing team can help you find a floor plan that fits your schedule and budget.

Location

Set where downtown meets Midtown, the neighborhood mixes converted warehouses, new mid-rise homes, and a steady lineup of restaurants and bars within a few blocks.

The light rail runs nearby for quick trips to the Museum District and the Medical Center, and the convention center and ballpark are an easy walk. Grocery, pharmacy, and dry cleaning are all close by.
